Thanks for trying this

This is a rough prototype of a game about getting through a month on not much money. We are testing the game, not you. There are no wrong moves. All the money and people are made up.

  • Play for about ten minutes. Try to reach the goal, and try anything else you are curious about.
  • If someone is with you, say what you are thinking as you go.
  • When you finish or get stuck, answer the short questions at the bottom.

How did that go?

Saving sends your answers, the steps you took in the game and any ideas you typed to the person running this test. Nothing else about you is collected. Please leave out anything private.
